How the figures are calculated

Extra Amount: the reported outlet total; if unavailable, the sum of recognized payment-channel extra amounts. Subtotal and grand-total rows are excluded.

Projection, target, saving and incentive: saved values from their source columns. The supplied workbook uses Extra Amount ÷ 29 × 31 for projection, a share of the ৳350,000 target, and Projection − Target − Extra Amount for Save Amount. Future files use their own saved values. Missing values remain unavailable.

Bank filtering: Extra Amount and channel charts use only selected banks. Projection, target and saving are unavailable for a bank-only view because the workbook does not split them by bank.

Leadership: exact normalized outlet-code match to Zone Distribution. Leader means RHO. Ambiguous or missing codes require review; names are not used for a speculative match.

Exclusions: excluded rows are removed from the active view and remain recoverable. Source totals and source files are retained.
